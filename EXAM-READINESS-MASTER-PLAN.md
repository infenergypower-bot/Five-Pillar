## Execution phases and closure criteria
1. Source and exam qualification: map every current objective to a direct technical source, version/availability, prerequisites, feature caveats and decision evidence; audit repository lessons and obsolete procedures.
2. Learning architecture: stable lesson IDs, explicit ordered objective map, prerequisite bridges, core objective checks, correct next/return links, no missed/duplicate objectives; validate internal lesson dependency and novice comprehension.
3. Foundations/vocabulary: atom-to-enterprise progression with definitions, examples, mechanisms, counterexamples and retrieval; audit all unexplained terms.
4. Technical depth: for each objective, explain mechanism, platform support, permissions/licensing, exact supported configuration, evidence, failure and alternatives. Review references and avoid unsupported claims.
5. Configuration/simulator: every supported scenario state-graded; test success, bad settings, bad scope, stale reports, conflicting policy, rollback, and persistence. Unsupported or premium actions remain design-only.
6. Scenarios/troubleshooting: varied realistic exhibits, logs and decisions; independently graded cases; document why wrong alternatives fail.
7. Exam bank: review each item and reference, eliminate ambiguous/predictable templates, reserve balanced unseen assessments, test grading, timing and exhaustion. Obtain independent SME validation; use official practice assessment separately.
8. Retention: varied retrieval after delays and cumulative interleaving; objective-specific weakness routing; verify state and clock limitations.
9. Real tenant: only authorized isolated eligible tenant and lab endpoints; perform, verify, redact and clean up each exercise; do not provision paid resources without approval. Real operations and learner performance cannot be simulated as proof.
10. Final QA and readiness: full regression, native target-browser/accessibility, current official exam and learner readiness evaluation. Never claim guarantee or label certification-ready until all applicable independent evidence exists.
